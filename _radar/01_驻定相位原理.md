## 1 结论与适用条件

考虑振荡积分

$$
I = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} a(t) e^{j\varphi(t)} \mathrm{d}t \tag{1}
$$

若满足：

*   $a(t)$ 是实包络，在驻点附近变化缓慢；
*   $\varphi(t)$ 是实相位，且变化足够快；
*   $\varphi(t)$ 在积分区间内存在唯一驻点 $t_0$（即 $\varphi^{\prime}(t_0) = 0$）且非退化（$\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0) \neq 0$）。

则积分的主要贡献集中在 $t_0$ 附近，近似值为

$$
I \approx a(t_0) e^{j\varphi(t_0)} \sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{\lvert \varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0) \rvert}} \exp\left( j\frac{\pi}{4} \operatorname{sgn}(\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)) \right) \tag{2}
$$

## 2 证明

非驻点区域因快速振荡相互抵消，只留 $t_0$ 附近长度为 $2\delta$ 的一段；在该段内把相位在驻点处展开，包络取常值：

$$
\varphi(t) = \varphi(t_0) + \frac{1}{2}\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)(t-t_0)^2 + O\left((t-t_0)^3\right),
\qquad
a(t) \approx a(t_0) \tag{3}
$$

略去高阶项并提取常数因子，得到

$$
I \approx a(t_0) e^{j\varphi(t_0)} \int_{t_0-\delta}^{t_0+\delta} \exp\left[ j\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)(t-t_0)^2 \right] \mathrm{d}t \tag{4}
$$

设 $\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0) > 0$。令 $\tau = t - t_0$，再令 $\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)\tau^2 = \frac{\pi}{2}y^2$，即

$$
\tau = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)}} y,
\qquad
Y = \sqrt{\frac{\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)}{\pi}} \delta \tag{5}
$$

被积函数是偶函数，于是

$$
I \approx 2a(t_0) e^{j\varphi(t_0)} \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)}} \int_{0}^{Y} e^{j\frac{\pi}{2}y^2} \mathrm{d}y \tag{6}
$$

标准菲涅尔积分给出 $\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{j\frac{\pi}{2}y^2} \mathrm{d}y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} e^{j\frac{\pi}{4}}$，而有限上限与无穷上限之差可以用分部积分估成

$$
\int_{Y}^{\infty} e^{j\frac{\pi}{2}y^2} \mathrm{d}y = \int_{Y}^{\infty} \frac{1}{j\pi y} \mathrm{d}\left( e^{j\frac{\pi}{2}y^2} \right) = \left[ \frac{e^{j\frac{\pi}{2}y^2}}{j\pi y} \right]_{Y}^{\infty} + \int_{Y}^{\infty} \frac{1}{j\pi y^2} e^{j\frac{\pi}{2}y^2} \mathrm{d}y = O\left(\frac{1}{Y}\right) \tag{7}
$$

只要 $\lvert \varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0) \rvert \delta^2 \gg 1$ 使 $Y$ 足够大，该项可忽略，积分上限延拓到无穷，代回式(6)：

$$
I \approx 2a(t_0) e^{j\varphi(t_0)} \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)}} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} e^{j\frac{\pi}{4}} = a(t_0) e^{j\varphi(t_0)} \sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)}} e^{j\frac{\pi}{4}} \tag{8}
$$

$\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0) < 0$ 时同样代换，指数变为 $-j\pi/4$。统一引入符号函数，即得式(2)。

## 3 应用：矩形包络 LFM 脉冲的频谱

取

$$
x(t) = \operatorname{rect}\left(\frac{t}{T_p}\right) e^{j\pi K t^2},
\qquad
B = \lvert K \rvert T_p \tag{8}
$$

$$
\operatorname{rect}\left(\frac{t}{T_p}\right) =
\begin{cases}
1, & \lvert t \rvert \le T_p/2 \\
0, & \lvert t \rvert > T_p/2
\end{cases}
\tag{9}
$$

按 $X(f) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} x(t) e^{-j2\pi f t} \mathrm{d}t$ 求频谱，总相位为 $\varphi(t) = \pi K t^2 - 2\pi f t$，令

$$
\varphi^{\prime}(t) = 2\pi K t - 2\pi f = 0 \tag{10}
$$

得到

$$
t_0 = \frac{f}{K} \tag{11}
$$

把 $t_0$ 代回总相位，得到

$$
\varphi(t_0) = \pi K \left(\frac{f}{K}\right)^2 - 2\pi f \cdot \frac{f}{K} = -\pi\frac{f^2}{K} \tag{12}
$$

此处 $\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0) = 2\pi K$ 与 $f$ 无关，且
$$a(t_0) = \operatorname{rect}\left(\dfrac{f}{K T_p}\right) = \operatorname{rect}\left(\dfrac{f}{B}\right) $$

把 $\varphi(t_0)$、$\varphi^{\prime\prime}(t_0)$、$a(t_0)$ 一起代入式(2)，得到

$$
X(f) \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{\lvert K \rvert}} \exp\left( j\frac{\pi}{4} \operatorname{sgn}(K) \right) \cdot \operatorname{rect}\left(\frac{f}{B}\right) \cdot \exp\left( -j\pi \frac{f^2}{K} \right) \tag{13}
$$

常系数通常不关心，直接省略，就得到 LFM 脉冲的频谱：

$$
\boxed{\, X(f) \approx \operatorname{rect}\left(\frac{f}{B}\right) \exp\left( -j\pi \frac{f^2}{K} \right) \,} \tag{14}
$$
